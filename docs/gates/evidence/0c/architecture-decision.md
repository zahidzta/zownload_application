The [0C-01 audit](library-audit.md) found that youtubedl-android 0.18.1's [library and ffmpeg POMs](https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/io/github/junkfood02/youtubedl-android/library/0.18.1/library-0.18.1.pom) ([ffmpeg POM](https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/io/github/junkfood02/youtubedl-android/ffmpeg/0.18.1/ffmpeg-0.18.1.pom)) declare GPL-3.0; its tagged arm64 FFmpeg embeds `--enable-gpl`, x264 and x265. Its bundled yt-dlp/ejs/QuickJS versions differ from Gate 0B's pins, and its [updater](https://github.com/yausername/youtubedl-android/blob/d725d5c9a18c3a99a13ee0308bf78275dc310760/library/src/main/java/com/yausername/youtubedl_android/YoutubeDLUpdater.kt#L16-L108) does not establish checksum verification or rollback after an accepted bad update. [FFmpeg's licence checklist](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) explains the LGPL build constraint. The Gate 0C stop rule remains in force.
