# Offline preview image

`single-video-thumbnail.jpg` is an unmodified 330-pixel JPEG preview of the video *10號風...* by vgocyl. Wikimedia Commons [hosts the video and preview](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:10%E8%99%9F%E9%A2%A8....webm) and records its reviewed [CC BY 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The original video is [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uW13KuRrg1g).

The preview is bundled only for the offline simulator. Its own license is CC BY 3.0; the surrounding application source is GPL-3.0-or-later. No image transformation was applied to the bundled JPEG.

`blender-playlist-cover.jpg` is the saved 336 × 188 thumbnail for the [Official Blender Open Movies playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6B3937A5D230E335). The Gate 0B playlist metadata associates this cover with *Spring* (`WhWc3b3KhnY`). `blender-first-entry-cover.jpg` is the saved 336 × 188 thumbnail for its first entry, [*Singularity*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l5OZu-IrXpw). Both JPEGs were obtained from the exact `i.ytimg.com` URLs recorded in `work/engine/cli/analyze/playlist.stdout.txt` and bundled unmodified so the simulator needs no network request.
